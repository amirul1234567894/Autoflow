n8n vs Zapier vs Make:
Which Is Best for Indian Businesses?

April 5, 20256 min readBy AutoFlowa Team

Zapier, Make, and n8n are the three most popular automation tools, and they all do the same core job: connect your apps and run tasks automatically. But they are built very differently, and the right choice for a business in India often comes down to one thing, cost in rupees versus dollars.

Here is an honest, no-fluff comparison to help you choose.

The Three Tools in One Line Each

  • Zapier, the easiest to start with, the largest app library, and the most expensive at scale. Fully cloud-based.
  • Make, a visual, flowchart-style tool that is more affordable than Zapier and handles complex multi-step flows well. Also cloud-based.
  • n8n, open-source and self-hostable, the cheapest to run at scale, and the most flexible, but it asks for a little more technical setup.

Cost: The Deciding Factor in India

This is where the gap really shows. Zapier and Make both charge in US dollars and bill by the number of tasks or operations you run. As your automations grow, so does the monthly bill, and the rupee conversion stings.

n8n flips the model. The software is free; you only pay for a small server to run it on. That means a fixed, predictable cost of roughly ₹200–500 per month no matter how many workflows or runs, which is a huge advantage for a growing Indian business.

💡 Rule of thumb: If you run only a handful of simple automations, a cloud tool's free tier may be enough. The moment you scale up to many workflows or high volumes, self-hosted n8n almost always becomes the cheaper option by a wide margin.

Ease of Use

Zapier wins for absolute beginners, you can build a basic automation in minutes with almost no learning curve. Make is slightly more involved but rewards you with more powerful, branching flows. n8n sits in the middle on usability but offers the most control once you are comfortable; for non-technical owners this is exactly where a setup partner helps.

Data Privacy

With Zapier and Make, your data passes through their servers. With self-hosted n8n, your data stays on your own server start to finish. For businesses handling customer details, that ownership and control is a genuine benefit.

Quick Comparison

Pricing  Zapier: USD, per task | Make: USD, per op | n8n: fixed server cost
Best for Zapier: beginners | Make: complex flows | n8n: scale + privacy
Hosting  Zapier: cloud | Make: cloud | n8n: self-host or cloud
Data     Zapier: their servers | Make: their servers | n8n: yours

Note: exact pricing for each tool changes over time, so always check the current plans before deciding.

So, Which Should You Choose?

  • Just testing the waters with 1–2 simple automations? Start with Zapier or Make's free tier.
  • Running a real business with growing automation needs? n8n gives you the best long-term cost and full data control.
  • Want power without the technical setup? Self-hosted n8n, set up for you once, is the sweet spot for most Indian businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I move from Zapier to n8n later?

Yes. Many businesses start on a cloud tool and switch to n8n once their bill grows. The workflows have to be rebuilt, but the logic carries over directly.

Is n8n really free?

The self-hosted community edition is free and open-source. Your only cost is the server it runs on. There is also a paid cloud version if you prefer not to manage hosting.
