10 Business Tasks You Can
Automate Today

March 21, 20256 min readBy AutoFlowa Team

You do not need a big team or a big budget to run a smoother business. Most of the work that drains your day is repetitive and rule-based, which means a tool like n8n can take it off your plate. Here are ten tasks you can start automating today, roughly in the order most businesses find useful.

1. Capturing Leads

Every form submission, Telegram message, or DM can be saved automatically to one Google Sheet, with the source and time attached. No more leads scattered across apps.

2. Replying Instantly to Enquiries

An auto-reply that fires the moment someone reaches out buys you time and reassures the customer. Even a simple "Thanks, we will get back within an hour" improves how responsive you seem.

3. Following Up

Most sales are lost simply because no one followed up. A workflow can send a polite nudge a day or two after the first contact, automatically, so warm leads never go cold.

4. Invoice and Payment Reminders

A scheduled check on your invoice tracker sends gentle reminders before and after the due date, so you get paid faster without the awkward chasing.

5. Appointment Confirmations and Reminders

Confirm bookings instantly and send a reminder the day before. This one change alone cuts no-shows noticeably for service businesses.

6. Moving Data Between Apps

Copying a lead from a form into your CRM, then into a sheet, then into your billing tool is the definition of a task a computer should do. n8n connects them so the data flows by itself.

7. Daily and Weekly Reports

Instead of building a summary by hand, have one delivered to your inbox or Telegram: yesterday's orders, this week's leads, totals at a glance.

8. Scheduling Social Posts

Queue content once and let a workflow publish it on schedule, keeping your presence consistent without daily manual posting.

9. Onboarding New Customers

When someone signs up, automatically send the welcome message, share the next steps, and create their folder or record, the same flawless first impression every time.

10. Asking for Reviews

A few days after a sale, an automatic, friendly message asking for a review or referral turns happy customers into growth, without you remembering to ask.

💡 Where to start: Pick the one task that annoys you most or costs you the most leads. Automate that first, feel the relief, then add the next. You do not have to do all ten at once.
